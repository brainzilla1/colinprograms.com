Last Updated: January 1, 2026

We do not collect any personal information. Our apps do not require user registration (emails), do not access your device's camera or microphone, and do not track your location.

Our apps do not contain any telemetry or analytics software. We do not track how you use the app, and no data regarding your usage is sent to our servers or any third-party services.

Our applications do not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
